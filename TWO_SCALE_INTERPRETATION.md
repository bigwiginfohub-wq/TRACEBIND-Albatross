\# TRACEBIND-Albatross: Two-Scale Interpretation of Coherence



Based on empirical cohort analysis of 20 independent ERA5 cases, the TRACEBIND descriptor framework operates on two distinct, complementary spatial scales. This distinction is critical for physical interpretation.



\---



\## 1. Local Cφ (Microstructure)

\*\*Definition:\*\* Computed via Derived Procedure A1 (sliding window), where each window's $C\_\\phi$ is referenced to its own geometric midpoint.

\*\*Physical Meaning:\*\* Measures whether a small, localized neighborhood (e.g., \~250 km × 250 km) exhibits rotational organization.

\*\*Empirical Finding:\*\* Across diverse meteorological regimes, the distribution of Local $C\_\\phi$ is remarkably stable (Mean ≈ 0.62–0.64, Std ≈ 0.03–0.05). Even storms with very low global coherence contain isolated pockets of high local coherence.



\---



\## 2. Global Cφ (Mesoscale Organization)

\*\*Definition:\*\* Computed over the entire domain, referenced to a single, estimated circulation center (e.g., maximum absolute vorticity).

\*\*Physical Meaning:\*\* Measures whether the locally coherent microstructures are \*collectively aligned\* and organized around a single, dominant geometric center.

\*\*Empirical Finding:\*\* Global $C\_\\phi$ exhibits a massive dynamic range (0.43 to 0.93 across the cohort). 



\---



\## The Unifying Hypothesis



The dramatic variation in Global $C\_\\phi$ is \*\*not\*\* driven by the \*existence\* of local coherence, but by the \*geometric alignment\* of that coherence.



\- \*\*High Global $C\_\\phi$ Storms:\*\* Contain locally coherent regions that are spatially clustered and collectively oriented around the global circulation center. The local variance is low because the organization is uniform.

\- \*\*Low Global $C\_\\phi$ Storms:\*\* Contain similar (or even stronger) isolated pockets of local coherence, but these pockets are scattered, competing, or oriented around \*different\* implied centers. The lack of a shared geometric reference causes the global tangential alignment to collapse.



This framework elevates TRACEBIND from a simple "coherence detector" to a tool that explicitly disentangles local kinematic microstructure from domain-scale geometric organization.
