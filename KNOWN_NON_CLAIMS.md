\# TRACEBIND: Known Non-Claims



This document explicitly enumerates what the TRACEBIND framework does \*\*NOT\*\* currently claim to achieve. This prevents over-interpretation and maintains scientific discipline.



TRACEBIND does \*\*NOT\*\* currently claim:

\- ✗ Improved aircraft fuel economy or propulsion efficiency.

\- ✗ Improved weather forecasting or turbulence prediction.

\- ✗ Discovery of new atmospheric physics or fluid dynamics laws.

\- ✗ Universal applicability across all spatial domains without independent validation.



\*\*Current Validated Claim:\*\*

TRACEBIND computes structural and kinematic descriptors whose statistical behavior has been rigorously tested under specific, documented validation protocols (e.g., Phase 8 C2 blinded trials). Any engineering utility (e.g., routing) remains a hypothesis to be tested, not an established fact.
